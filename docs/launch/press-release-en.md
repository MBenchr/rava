# ISANDRE introduces ṬĀQA, furniture that lets the room continue

**INTERNAL RELEASE DRAFT — DO NOT DISTRIBUTE BEFORE CLEARANCE**

**Standfirst.** Three open forms give objects a place while preserving light,
movement and sightlines.

`[CITY, APPROVED PUBLICATION DATE]` — ISANDRE introduces ṬĀQA, its first
collection of open furniture. The collection begins with a domestic
observation: objects need a place, yet the furniture that holds them can easily
become another wall. ṬĀQA is drawn around openings instead. Each piece gives
the room structure while allowing daylight, movement and conversation to
continue through it.

The family is deliberately concise. SEUIL 01 is a tall open cabinet measuring
102 × 184 × 42 cm. It can mark a passage or stand away from a wall without
closing the view. PORTÉE 02 turns the same visual language horizontally at
184 × 102 × 42 cm, forming a low line between living, dining or working zones.
VEILLE 03 brings the idea beside the bed in a smaller form with two open
compartments. VEILLE’s final dimensions will be published only after the
manufacturer drawing has been approved.

The three pieces share rounded edges, a continuous base and openings that
remain visually active from both sides. Their asymmetry is not decorative: it
creates distinct places for books, ceramics, a glass or the small objects that
move through daily life. The collection is intended to be recognisable empty
and to become more personal as it is lived with.

Four finishes shape four atmospheres without changing the silhouette. Chalk is
quiet and architectural. Butter catches warm daylight. Sage connects the form
to stone, wood and plants. Rose Clay gives the object a softer presence at the
end of the day. Physical colour and surface references will replace digital
approvals after the golden sample process is complete.

Indicative catalogue prices begin at €3,000 for SEUIL and PORTÉE and €750 for
VEILLE. Each finish has a fixed catalogue price. Market currency, tax,
delivery and eligibility are calculated before payment. Production is planned
to begin after a confirmed order, with a target preparation time of 20 working
days; the final public promise depends on the approved manufacturing and
transport trials.

The online experience keeps the buying decision close to the product. Visitors
can compare the three pieces, switch finishes, see the exact price, review
scale and open-back views, and continue to secure checkout without creating an
account. A secondary room-view tool lets them test SEUIL or PORTÉE in a
photograph of their interior. The simulation is presented as an indicative
view, not as dimensional or material proof.

ISANDRE was conceived in France as an independent design house focused on the
relationship between furniture and the room around it. The first chapter is
not built around artificial scarcity or borrowed heritage. Its credibility
will come from a consistent form, exact information, physical samples,
documented delivery and, over time, real homes and service records.

Public availability, launch territories and the final delivery policy will be
announced only after legal, industrial and logistics gates have been signed
off. Press images will identify whether they are digital campaign images or
physical documentary photography. No image of an atelier, maker, installation
or owner will be represented without a real subject and the necessary rights.
