# RFQ A5 — ISANDRE / ṬĀQA furniture

Reference: `ISR-A5-RFQ-001`  
Issue: 27 July 2026  
Commercial status: confidential request for quotation  
Production status: prototype required, not released for series production

## 1. Purpose

ISANDRE requests a comparable technical and commercial proposal for the
development of SEUIL 01 and PORTÉE 02. The supplier must price the requested
scope without redesigning the approved silhouette.

Primary route: mass-coloured furniture-grade rotationally moulded LLDPE.  
Fallback route: GFRP with a qualified mineral finish, quoted separately.

VEILLE 03 is excluded from production pricing until its canonical dimensions
are approved.

## 2. Confidential package

The issued package must be listed in the supplier response:

- geometry manifest version and checksum ;
- dimensioned external and opening drawings ;
- approved front, three-quarter and rear reference views ;
- surface and colour coupon specification ;
- origin-plate keep-out and insert zone ;
- declared-use and test brief ;
- packaging brief ;
- this RFQ revision.

No subcontracting, file sharing or tooling transfer is permitted without prior
written approval. The quotation must state ownership, storage, maintenance and
return terms for every tool.

## 3. Immutable product definition

| Product | W × H × D | Openings | Visual section | Plinth | Status |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| SEUIL 01 | 1020 × 1840 × 420 mm | 8 | 80 mm | 100 mm, 25 mm recess | prototype |
| PORTÉE 02 | 1840 × 1020 × 420 mm | 8 | 80 mm | 100 mm, 25 mm recess | prototype |
| VEILLE 03 | not issued | 2 visual | not issued | not issued | blocked |

Mandatory characteristics:

- open front and rear, with every opening fully through ;
- no back panel, doors, handles, visible feet or visible fasteners ;
- equal-quality front, rear, side and inner returns ;
- continuous base and one coherent family of radii ;
- no added or removed opening ;
- no reduction of the visual section ;
- no decorative imitation of stone, concrete, marble or terrazzo.

Any requested geometry change must be returned as a marked DFM proposal and
must never be applied to a prototype without written approval.

## 4. Route A — rotationally moulded LLDPE

The supplier shall propose:

- furniture-grade resin, mass-coloured and traceable by batch ;
- nominal `7 mm` skin and a proposed contractual local minimum ;
- mould split, draft, vent, trimming and cooling strategy ;
- hidden kiss-offs, returns or ribs required for stiffness ;
- inserts and removable ballast interface ;
- hidden adjustable non-slip feet ;
- two concealed anti-tip interfaces for SEUIL ;
- correction jigs and dimensional-control method ;
- recyclability and repair strategy ;
- cycle time, expected scrap and colour-change loss.

The surface target is nearly smooth and low eggshell, initially `8–15 GU at
60°`. No joint, vent, sink, pinhole or trimming trace may remain visible on
A-surfaces or normal viewing angles.

## 5. Route B — fallback composite

Quote separately:

- GFRP skin and core architecture ;
- local aluminium reinforcement ;
- structural bond and joint-concealment system ;
- compatible low-sheen mineral finish ;
- repair process and colour-matched repair kit ;
- VOC, curing and fire-performance documentation ;
- mould life and manual-hours sensitivity.

The fallback is assessed only if the LLDPE prototype fails a hard gate on
geometry, stiffness, creep, sound, touch, surface, repair or total landed cost.

## 6. Required prototype sequence

1. colour and surface coupons ;
2. full-scale radius, niche edge, joint/trim and plate-insert witnesses ;
3. functional section with a `640 mm` span, loaded for 30 days ;
4. SEUIL first article ;
5. PORTÉE first article after SEUIL gate review ;
6. packaging pilot ;
7. laboratory and real-route transport tests ;
8. golden sample ;
9. pilot lot ;
10. series release.

The supplier must not combine these gates into one unreviewed production step.

## 7. Quotation structure

Quote in EUR, excluding VAT, for each product and route:

| Cost line | Required response |
|---|---|
| DFM / engineering | fixed price, hours, deliverables |
| master / pattern | fixed price and ownership |
| mould / tooling | fixed price, life, storage, maintenance |
| coupons | price per family and lead time |
| functional section | price and lead time |
| first article | price and lead time |
| unit ex-works | 10 / 50 / 100 / 250 / 500 units |
| colour change | fixed and variable loss |
| reinforcement / inserts | separate unit cost |
| ballast cassette | 10–15 kg and 20–25 kg options |
| adjustable feet / anti-tip kit | separate unit cost |
| origin plate preparation | insert, keep-out and assembly only |
| packaging | unit cost and packed dimensions/weight |
| QC | included checks and paid options |
| laboratory testing | allowance or third-party quote |
| repair / touch-up kit | unit cost and process |
| freight | clearly stated Incoterm and route |

No value may be presented as landed cost unless it includes packaging, tool
amortisation, QC, freight, insurance, duties, handling, warehouse receipt,
damage allowance, repair and financing assumptions.

## 11. Award rule

Award is based on hard gates, golden sample, total landed cost and repeatable
quality. The lowest ex-works quote does not win automatically.

No purchase order for series production is issued before signed approval of
geometry, tests, packaging and golden sample.

## Résumé fournisseur francophone

La consultation porte sur SEUIL 01 et PORTÉE 02 sans modification de forme. La
voie principale est le LLDPE rotomoulé teinté masse ; le composite est un devis
de repli séparé. Les prix doivent être fournis à `10, 50, 100, 250 et 500`
unités, avec DFM, moule, premier article, emballage, contrôle et logistique
séparés. VEILLE 03 reste hors prix tant que ses dimensions ne sont pas validées.
