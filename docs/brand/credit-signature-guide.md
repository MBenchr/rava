## Image record

Every published master stores:

- asset ID and version;
- product, finish and geometry-kit version;
- photographer or generation workflow;
- stylist;
- architecture and location when creditable;
- usage rights and expiry;
- AI-generation disclosure in embedded metadata when applicable;
- approval status and approving person;
- crop derivatives and channels.

## Public credit order

```text
Photography · NAME
Styling · NAME
Architecture · NAME / STUDIO
Location · NAME, CITY
```

Omit an unavailable role instead of inventing it. Owner names, workshop names,
manufacturing sites and country-of-origin claims are used only with written
evidence and permission.

## Press assets

Press filenames use:

```text
isandre_taqa_product_finish_scene_asset-id_credit.ext
```

Captions state whether the image is a campaign visual, a real installation or
a manufacturing proof. Generated campaign visuals never illustrate claims
about real owners, workshops, artisans or provenance.
