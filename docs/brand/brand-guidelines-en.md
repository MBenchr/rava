# ISANDRE / ṬĀQA - Digital and physical identity

Version: A4.1 - 27 July 2026  
Status: digital system implemented; legal and physical gates remain open

## Architecture

| Level | Name | Role |
|---|---|---|
| House | ISANDRE | Permanent publishing and design house |
| Collection | ṬĀQA | Editorial name; `TAQA` in technical systems |
| Products | SEUIL 01 / PORTÉE 02 / VEILLE 03 | Fixed product identities |

Permanent signature: `THE ROOM CONTINUES.`  
Promise: `Furniture that structures space without closing it.`

`ṬĀQA` means a niche, cupboard or opening made within the thickness of a wall.
The collection translates the gesture, not a decorative style. The Arabic
spelling is not a second logo and requires specialist validation before use.

## Wordmark

The primary mark is the custom capital wordmark `ISANDRE`. It is independent
from the collection and contains no arch.

- Minimum screen width: `90 px`.
- Minimum print width: `18 mm`.
- Clear space: at least three `I` widths horizontally and two vertically.
- Use only Ink, Limewash, bronze or a material reserve.

## L'ENTAILLE

The secondary sign is a dense block from which one square is removed.

- Overall ratio: `1:1.55`.
- Notch: `0.34 x 0.34` of sign width.
- Notch centre: `62%` of sign height.
- No radius, perspective, second notch, outline, shadow or pattern repetition.

## Typography

- Wordmark: custom vector capitals.
- Editorial display: Bodoni Moda.
- Interface and body: Manrope.
- References: Manrope Medium with tabular numerals.

Titles use sentence case. Collection signatures, labels and references use
spaced capitals. The wordmark is never recreated with a font.

## Layout and interface

- Twelve columns on desktop; four on mobile.
- Asymmetry is allowed, misalignment is not.
- Images behave as openings rather than decorated cards.
- Controls are `48-52 px`; control radii are `0-2 px`.
- Image radii never exceed `16 px`.
- No cosmetic pills, arch icons or permanent decorative effects.
- Motion lasts `240-400 ms` and never morphs product geometry.

## Photography

Use one readable light source, grazing light on thickness and open shadows
inside every niche. Pair frontal recognition with a side or rear proof. Show
real, sourced domestic objects with credible scale and gravity.

Do not fabricate workshops, artisans, owners, press, origin or production
evidence. Generative campaign images create desire; real images create proof.
Their status and credits remain distinct.

## Origin plate

`La Marque d'origine` is a full `42.07 x 26.00 mm` rectangle in dark silicon
bronze, brushed horizontally and installed flush. It sits on the inner right
return of the lower horizontal opening, `28-32 mm` behind the front edge.

Working inscription:

```text
ISANDRE
ṬĀQA · SEUIL 01
DESIGN · MOHYI BENCHRIH
NO 000127
```

`FRANCE` appears only after legally proven origin. NFC remains concealed and is
approved only after a ferrite and five-phone RF test.

## Authentication and packaging

The serial number is unique, repeated discreetly in the product and linked to a
persistent product passport. The passport records model, finish, batch, actual
production site, quality checks, care, repairs and optional ownership transfer.

Primary packaging is Limewash or natural unbleached board with Ink wordmark,
one Passage Cobalt collection rule and a technical label on one side. No product
photograph is printed on the primary carton.

## Prohibited uses

- Do not combine `ISANDRE TAQA` as one compound house name.
- Do not use `TAKA`; it is archived.
- Do not add an arch, pseudo-Arabic lettering or generic Moroccan motifs.
- Do not repeat L'ENTAILLE as decoration.
- Do not use mirror gold, systematic shine or a fashion-thin serif.
- Do not write `ṬĀQA` in URLs, SKUs or systems without full Unicode support.
- Do not claim French manufacture, popularity, scarcity, press or provenance
  without evidence.
- Do not change a product geometry to improve an image composition.
